### Note on Metric Selection
An earlier iteration of this Pareto chart ranked sub-categories by `total_discount_given`,
which surfaced high-ticket categories (Phones, Chairs) that remain net-profitable — an
artifact of discount *amount* scaling with unit price, not with actual margin risk. 
Re-ranking by actual dollar loss (`sum of negative Profit`) correctly surfaces the
low-margin sub-categories (Paper, Binders, Furnishings, Art) that drive real business
impact: the top 4 sub-categories alone account for 52.3% of total dollar losses, and
the top 6 account for ~75%, closely approaching the classic 80/20 threshold.
