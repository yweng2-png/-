### Customer Segmentation Insights

Four segments emerged from KMeans (k=4), each with distinct, actionable profiles:

- **High-Value, Low-Satisfaction (6.4%)**: Spend ~12-15x the average ($1,073 vs ~$70-87)
  but report the lowest satisfaction (3.3 vs 3.7 elsewhere) — the highest-priority segment
  for retention intervention, given their outsized revenue contribution.
- **High-Frequency Loyalists (15.9%)**: Purchase nearly weekly (Freq_Score=52, ~4.5x the
  rate of other segments) despite modest spend per visit — an established repeat-purchase
  habit best supported through loyalty/membership programs rather than acquisition spend.
- **Young Low-Frequency (38.8%) and Older Low-Frequency (38.9%)**: Together 77.7% of the
  customer base, both purchasing roughly quarterly with similar spend levels — differ
  primarily by age (30.8 vs 57.2), suggesting frequency (not spend capacity) is the
  binding constraint and the more promising lever for activation campaigns.
